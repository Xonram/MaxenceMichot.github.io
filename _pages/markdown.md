## About mathematics

 * [On proof and progress in mathematics](/files/proofandprogress.pdf), William Thurston. In an age where LLM's can produce thousands of pages of theorems "easily" , the questions "What is it that mathematicians do ?", "What is the goal of mathematics ?" or "What constitutes progress in mathematics?" are questions that I think should be asked more often. This 30-page paper tackles some of these questions, and is readable by both the mathematician and the non-mathematician.
