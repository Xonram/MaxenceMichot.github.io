Hello, I'm Maxence. I'm a mathematics PhD student in [Université de Nantes](https://www.univ-nantes.fr/) under the supervision of [Elise Goujard](https://www.math.sciences.univ-nantes.fr/Elise-Goujard#accueil) and [Duc-Manh Nguyen](https://www.idpoisson.fr/ducmanhnguyen/). My research is on moduli spaces of *k*-differentials.

More specifically, my thesis focuses on computing volumes of these spaces, and on studying combinatorial, algebraic and probabilistic questions on these *k*-differentials.

My broader mathematical center of interests are the fields in which visuals and drawings play a key role. They personally help me a lot understanding what's going on. This concerns most of geometry, but is not limited to it.

Outside of my research area, I am interested in rock climbing, piano, philosophy of mathematics and science communication, especially for those that the scientific institutions often leave at bay.
